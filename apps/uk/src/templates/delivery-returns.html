UK delivery: Processing and transit

Standard tracked delivery to all UK addresses is free on every order. Orders normally take 1–3 business days to process. After dispatch, standard tracked transit is usually 7–20 business days.

Delivery dates are guidelines rather than guarantees. Weekends, bank holidays, weather events, address verifications, and carrier disruption can occasionally impact timings.

When your order is dispatched, a tracking ID and carrier link are emailed to the address provided at checkout (tracking updates usually appear within 24–72 hours). You can also monitor your package directly using the tracking link in your email dispatch confirmation. For complete transit details, view our full Shipping Policy.

Order changes, cancellations and delays

If you need to amend a shipping address, please contact us within 2 hours of order placement. If you wish to request an order cancellation, you must contact us within 12 hours of placing the order before fulfillment begins. Email support@trymiroooo.com or submit our Contact Form immediately. Because orders enter automated fulfilment rapidly, requests submitted within these windows cannot be guaranteed once an order has entered processing.

If tracking has not updated for several business days or the estimated transit window has passed, send us your order number, checkout email, and delivery postcode so our support team can investigate directly with the courier.

Returns & refunds

Returns may be requested within 30 days of delivery strictly for damaged, defective, incorrect, or missing products. Customers must provide clear photo or video evidence and obtain written return authorization and instructions from our support team before sending anything back. Items must not be returned to the address shown on the parcel label. Change-of-mind, incorrect-size, or personal-preference returns are not generally accepted.

Unless the item is faulty, incorrectly supplied, or applicable consumer law provides otherwise, return postage is the customer’s responsibility. We recommend using a tracked postal service and retaining proof of postage until your return is completed.

Please return the handle, travel case, included accessories, USB-C charging cable, and original components supplied with your order. Consumable replacement brush heads supplied in separate sealed packaging must remain unopened unless they were part of the initial set being returned.

Important return notice: Do not send items back to an address printed on the delivery parcel label without contacting us first. Fulfilment depots and returns processing centres differ, and unauthorised returns can be delayed or lost.

Refunds: Inspection and repayment

Once your authorised return is received and inspected at our returns facility, we will email you to confirm the outcome. Approved refunds are credited directly to the original payment method.

Approved refunds are initiated within 5–10 business days. Your bank or card provider may require additional time to reflect the funds on your statement once the refund is issued.

We may make a reasonable deduction if returned goods show excessive cosmetic damage beyond what is reasonably necessary to evaluate the product. This policy is in addition to, and does not limit, your statutory rights under UK consumer law.

Problems on arrival: Faulty or damaged items

Please inspect your package promptly upon arrival. If an item is damaged in transit, defective, or not what you ordered, contact us right away with your order number, a brief explanation, and photos or video of the issue.
