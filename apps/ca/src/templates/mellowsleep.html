    One Pillow. Three Zones. Zero Compromises

    Say goodbye to flat pillows, awkward angles, and one-size-fits-all shapes. CloudAlign’s SwitchFit™ design gives you dual-height support, ergonomic zones, and custom comfort. No shifting, bunching, or second-guessing.

    Side Sleeper Zone

    Side wings relieve pressure on your shoulder and keep your neck elevated at the right angle — no bunching, no arm pain.

    Lift Side (SwitchFit™ Design)

    Designed for deeper support and lift, this side is perfect for side sleepers or anyone who needs extra neck height and pressure relief.

    Soft Side (SwitchFit™ Design)

    Softer and lower, this side works best for back and stomach sleepers or those who prefer a more relaxed head position and minimal elevation.

    The Full Sleep System

    Every zone works together so your neck, spine, and shoulders stay supported no matter how you sleep. Flip to your preferred side, settle into the contoured zones, and let the SwitchFit™ design do the rest. One pillow, fully dialed in, all night long.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about the CloudAlign Pillow.

    How does the dual-height system work?

    Each side of the pillow is different in height, one higher and one lower. Flip the pillow to find the support level that feels best for your neck and sleep position.

    Is this pillow good for side, back, and stomach sleepers?

    Yes. CloudAlign is designed for all sleep styles. Its ergonomic shape adapts to side, back, and stomach sleepers by providing targeted lift and pressure relief where you need it most.

    Will it flatten over time?

    No. CloudAlign is made with shape-retaining materials that keep their support even after frequent washing.

    Is it safe for sensitive skin?

    Absolutely. The OEKO-TEX® certified cover is hypoallergenic, breathable, and gentle on skin.
