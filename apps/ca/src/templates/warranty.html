Product Care

Daily maintenance & handling.

Miroooo electric toothbrushes are engineered for daily oral care. To keep your handle and brush heads in optimal condition, rinse the brush head thoroughly after each use and allow it to dry upright in an open area. Wipe the handle with a soft, damp cloth as needed, and ensure the charging port is completely clean and dry before connecting the USB-C cable.

Consumable brush heads should be replaced every 3 months for optimal plaque removal and hygiene.

Troubleshooting

Common product guidance.

To prevent premature wear or performance issues, please observe the following care tips:

  • Avoid charging the device while wet or submerged;
  • Do not use abrasive cleaning agents, harsh chemicals, or boiling water on the handle or brush heads;
  • Avoid dropping or subjecting the handle to excessive physical impact;
  • Use standard certified 5V USB power adapters for charging;
  • Do not attempt to open, disassemble, or self-repair the internal motor and battery casing.
