Tracking FAQ

We offer free tracked Australia delivery. Orders are processed within 1 to 3 business days and shipped with Australia Post Express. Once dispatched, transit usually takes 3 to 7 business days.

Please note: Your order may split into multiple shipments when it includes a pre-ordered or back-ordered item. If this occurs, you will receive tracking details for each separate shipment.
