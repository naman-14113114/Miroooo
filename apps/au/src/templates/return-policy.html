1. About This Policy

This Policy applies to purchases from Miroooo Australia.

In this Policy:

  • Store means Miroooo Australia.
  • xPage Drop means xPage Drop LLC.
  • Seller means the independent merchant operating the Store.
  • Fulfilment Partner means a supplier, manufacturer, warehouse or carrier.
  • You means the buyer.
  • We, us and our mean the Store and xPage Drop where it handles the request.

2. Contact Support First

Before cancelling or returning an order, contact:

support@trymiroooo.com or Contact Us

You must receive written return authorisation before sending a product. The approved return address may differ from:

  1. The shipping origin.
  2. The address printed on the package.
  3. The Fulfilment Partner's address.
  4. The xPage Drop corporate address.

Do not return products to any address without written authorisation.

3. Order Cancellations

Cancellation requests may be submitted within:

Cancellation period: Cancellation requests must be submitted within 12 hours of placing the order. Cancellation is only possible if fulfilment has not started. Requests submitted within this period are not guaranteed once the order has entered processing.

We will try to stop the order, but cancellation cannot be guaranteed after payment review, preparation, customisation, packaging or fulfilment begins.

If cancellation succeeds, the cancelled amount will be refunded to the original payment method. After fulfilment begins, you may need to receive the order and request a return.

4. Change-of-Mind Returns

Change-of-mind returns may be requested within:

Return window: Within 30 days of delivery

Returned products must meet these conditions:

Return condition: Returns are accepted for damaged, defective, incorrect or missing products, or where required under Australian Consumer Law. Customers must provide clear photo or video evidence and obtain return authorisation before sending anything back. Items must not be returned to the address shown on the package. Change-of-mind returns on personal oral hygiene items that have been unsealed or used are not accepted for health and safety reasons.

You may inspect a product as reasonably necessary, but you may be responsible for reduced value caused by use, damage or handling beyond normal inspection where permitted by law.

5. Defective, Damaged or Incorrect Products

Contact support promptly if a product:

  1. Is defective or unsafe.
  2. Arrives damaged.
  3. Is materially different from its description.
  4. Is the wrong item, size, colour or model.
  5. Is missing parts or quantities.
  6. Does not function as reasonably advertised.

We may request reasonable evidence such as photographs, measurements, packaging information or a short video showing the issue.

An unboxing video is not the only accepted evidence.

Depending on the issue and applicable law under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL), the remedy may include replacement, missing parts, repair, partial refund or full refund.

6. Missing Items and Lost Shipments

Check whether your order was divided into separate packages before reporting a missing item.

For a missing item, provide photographs of the received package, label and contents where reasonably available.

If an item was omitted or a shipment is confirmed lost, we will provide a replacement, refund or other remedy required by law.

7. Delayed Shipments

Delivery estimates are not guarantees unless expressly stated otherwise.

A reasonable delay does not automatically qualify for a refund. However, if delivery materially exceeds the promised period, you may receive a revised delivery option, cancellation or refund as required by law.

You are not required to accept an indefinite delay.

8. Return Shipping Costs

The applicable return-cost rule is:

Return shipping: Return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility unless xPage Drop LLC confirms otherwise in writing. Products must not be returned without prior authorisation and return instructions.

For validated defective, damaged, incorrect or materially misdescribed products, we will provide a prepaid method, reimburse reasonable authorised return costs or provide another remedy required by the Australian Consumer Law (ACL).

Unauthorised or unnecessarily expensive return shipping may not be reimbursed.

9. Return Inspection

Returned products may be inspected before a refund is approved. The inspection may confirm:

  1. The correct product was returned.
  2. The product is complete.
  3. The reported defect exists.
  4. The product was not intentionally damaged or replaced.
  5. The return conditions were followed.

A lawful deduction may be made for missing parts, unauthorised damage or excessive use. Normal inspection will not remove mandatory return rights.

10. Non-Returnable Products

Subject to mandatory law, the following products may not qualify for change-of-mind returns:

Non-returnable products: Products are non-returnable when the request is based on a change of mind for opened oral hygiene devices/brush heads, personal preference, an incorrectly selected variant, an incorrect shipping address, minor packaging imperfections without product damage, or failure to complete customs clearance.

This may include personalised, perishable, hygiene-sensitive or opened sealed products where legally permitted.

A non-returnable classification does not remove rights relating to defective, unsafe, incorrect or materially misdescribed products under the Australian Consumer Law.

11. Refunds

Approved refunds will normally be sent to the original payment method.

Refund initiation time: 5–10 business days

Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to display the refund on your account.

Where the original payment method cannot receive the refund, another lawful method may be requested.

Original delivery fees will be refunded where required by applicable law.

12. Partial Refunds and Replacements

A partial refund may be offered if you choose to keep a product with a minor issue.

A replacement may be offered for defective, damaged, incorrect, missing or lost products. A materially different replacement will not be sent without your approval.

A partial refund or replacement will not replace a remedy that applicable law requires.

13. Refused and Unauthorised Returns

Do not refuse a package or send a return without contacting support.

Unauthorised returns may be refused, lost or delayed. You may be responsible for avoidable costs resulting from failure to follow reasonable return instructions.

This does not remove mandatory consumer rights under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL).

14. Fraudulent or Abusive Claims

We may reject a claim involving materially false or misleading information, intentional damage, substitution of products, false non-delivery statements or duplicate recovery attempts.

Exercising a legitimate refund, consumer-protection or chargeback right is not abuse.

We may provide relevant order, delivery, refund and communication records to payment providers or competent authorities when fraud is reasonably suspected.

15. Chargebacks

Contact Miroooo Australia / xPage Drop support before opening a chargeback so the issue can be reviewed directly. This does not restrict legitimate chargeback rights.

Do not seek both a completed refund and a chargeback for the same amount. An active chargeback may pause a separate refund while the payment provider controls the disputed funds.

16. Mandatory Rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL). You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Where Australian Consumer Law grants mandatory rights, legal guarantees, or remedies, those provisions prevail over conflicting parts of this Policy.
