# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK shoppers who want a premium electric toothbrush that feels considered, travels well and is simple to understand. They are comparing Miroooo with established sonic toothbrushes and need to see the product, the difference between X and X2, the contents of the box, delivery expectations and support terms before buying.

## Product Purpose

Miroooo is a focused oral-care storefront for two electric toothbrush models, Miroooo X and Miroooo X2. The store should make model selection straightforward, carry visitors from discovery into the approved product pages, and keep the browsing experience under the Miroooo brand until the external payment step is configured. Success means visitors can understand the range, trust the store, reach the right product and find honest support or policy information without friction.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm and assured. Miroooo should feel like a well-made bathroom instrument rather than a loud gadget or a clinical appliance. Copy is concise, useful and written in natural UK English.

## Anti-references

- No inherited source-store identity, routes, metadata, analytics or customer-facing references.
- No generic white-and-teal dental-clinic aesthetic.
- No copied-store residue, Dutch language, placeholder contact details or broken Shopify routes.
- No fabricated reviews, medical claims, dentist endorsements, testing, scarcity or urgency.
- No generic AI landing-page card grids, purple gradients, glass effects or decorative clutter.

## Design Principles

1. Product first. Lead with the physical toothbrush, its material and its everyday use.
2. Two models, one clear decision. Explain X versus X2 without forcing visitors to compare long specifications unaided.
3. Premium through restraint. Use proportion, typography, product photography and motion rather than decoration.
4. Proof stays honest. Show real specifications and supplied media; do not manufacture authority or customer evidence.
5. Fast paths to purchase and support. Navigation, product links, policies and contact routes must work immediately on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic headings, descriptive alternative text, minimum 44px interactive targets, sufficient contrast and reduced-motion behaviour. Colour must never be the only signal for availability, selection or success.
